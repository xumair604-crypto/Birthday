A letter for 26 November

Dear Karuna,

I have written this letter in my head more times than I can count. This is the first time I am letting it out.

Eight years ago I noticed you, and something in me settled. I did not know it would last this long. I only knew your name felt different when I said it. Eight years later, it still does.

In all this time I have never stopped admiring you. The way you carry yourself. The calm you bring into a room without trying. The small things I learned to notice, because noticing you became a habit I never wanted to break.

You matter to me more than I have ever managed to say. You are the first thought on good days and the quiet comfort on hard ones. Somewhere along the way you became the measure I use for what kindness, grace and beauty look like.

Whatever you decide to do with this, I wanted you to know that for eight years someone has been quietly, steadily, completely glad that you exist.

Happy Birthday, Karuna. May this year give you everything you give to the world, and more.

With all my heart, always yours
